## 1. What changed in v2.1 (`audit/AUDIT_LOG.md`)
1. **Interaction masking.** Masked STAR3D target endpoints are now excluded, and each comparison has its own denominator.
   - Effect: guanidine-I target side 110 → 105 eligible interactions.
   - Additional defect: a symmetry-mate stack in 4KQY had been counted as an intrachain self-stack (4KQY 181 → 180).
2. **Region eligibility.** Eligibility now checks the whole source span and both methods' target partners, and
   correspondence disagreements are counted separately from coverage differences.
   - Effect: 1 of 19 regions changed (`RF00059…r17-23` is no longer eligible).
   - Reviewed interpretations are now carried forward instead of reset.
3. **STAR3D wrapper.** Any preprocessing or mount-check failure now stops the pair before alignment (tested against
   the old code, where it failed).
4. **Safeguards.**
   - Unmapped STAR3D lines or non-one-to-one mappings are now validation failures.
   - Subset reruns are refused.
   - FR3D caches carry provenance sidecars.
   - Fit anchors must be unmasked on both sides and nondegenerate.
5. **Evidence.**
   - Exact-source genome checks were run for 9 RNAs.
   - The candidate was rebuilt from raw files, and an exploratory adjustment was validated.
6. **Cohort v1.1.** RF00162 SAM-I was reclassified as *exploratory (engineered, technical)*. Both constructs are
   engineered inside the family interval (P3 and P4), and STAR3D aligned only the engineered P3. This reason is
   partly outcome-informed, and is flagged as such.
7. **Corrections.**
   - The earlier statement that 3FU2's paper numbering is offset from PDB numbering was wrong; they coincide (C12 has
     only phosphate atoms modelled; U13 and A14 have no coordinates).
   - "Method-neutral fit" has been renamed **shared-correspondence anchor fit**.

## 6. Limits
- Canonical-pair evidence is not independent of STAR3D, because STAR3D's preprocessing uses MC-Annotate WWc pairs.
- Consensus secondary structure alone cannot establish an error.
- The fresh rerun covers the preQ1 pairs only.
- STAR3D ran under QEMU emulation.
- FR3D labels its annotations as not finalized.
- Several construct papers could not be accessed.
- The cohort has 3 primary families, with pairs that share RNAs.
- No rates may be extrapolated.
