## 1. What changed in v2.1 (`audit/AUDIT_LOG.md`)
1. **Interaction masking.** Masked STAR3D target endpoints are now excluded, and each comparison has its own denominator.
   - Effect: guanidine-I target side 110 → 105 eligible interactions.
   - Additional defect: a symmetry-mate stack in 4KQY had been counted as an intrachain self-stack (4KQY 181 → 180).
2. **Region eligibility.** Eligibility now checks the whole source span and both methods' target partners, and
   correspondence disagreements are counted separately from coverage differences.
   - Effect: 1 of 19 regions changed (`RF00059…r17-23` is no longer eligible).
   - Reviewed interpretations are now carried forward instead of reset.
3. **STAR3D wrapper.** Any preprocessing or mount-check failure now stops the pair before alignment (tested against
   the old code, where it failed).
4. **Safeguards.**
   - Unmapped STAR3D lines or non-one-to-one mappings are now validation failures.
   - Subset reruns are refused.
   - FR3D caches carry provenance sidecars.
   - Fit anchors must be unmasked on both sides and nondegenerate.
5. **Evidence.**
   - Exact-source genome checks were run for 9 RNAs.
   - The candidate was rebuilt from raw files, and an exploratory adjustment was validated.
6. **Cohort v1.1.** RF00162 SAM-I was reclassified as *exploratory (engineered, technical)*. Both constructs are
   engineered inside the family interval (P3 and P4), and STAR3D aligned only the engineered P3. This reason is
   partly outcome-informed, and is flagged as such.
7. **Corrections.**
   - The earlier statement that 3FU2's paper numbering is offset from PDB numbering was wrong; they coincide (C12 has
     only phosphate atoms modelled; U13 and A14 have no coordinates).
   - "Method-neutral fit" has been renamed **shared-correspondence anchor fit**.

## 5. Candidate: RF00522, 7REX row, P1 3′ strand (`review/candidate_RF00522/`)
- **Sequences and provenance.**
  - 6VUI `CUGGGUCGCAGUAACCCCAGUUAACAAAACAAG` (row `URS000080E32E_119072/1-33`)
  - 3FU2 `AGAGGUUCUAGCUACACCCUCUAUAAAAAACUAA` (`URS000080E020_32630/1-34`)
  - 7REX `UGUGGUUCGCAACCAUCCCACAUAAAAAAACUAG` (`URS00023119CB_2126436/1-34`)

  Each family interval is the whole deposited chain, and each is exact in its source genome. None has modified
  nucleotides or engineering. Coordinate gaps: 3FU2 C12 has only phosphate atoms, and U13 and A14 are absent.
- **Numbering.** The chain from seed column to row index to label_seq_id to author number is in
  `numbering_chain.tsv`. For these chains, row index = label = author number.
- **P1 register.** The raw FR3D lines (`p1_raw_fr3d_lines.txt`) show 6VUI pairs 1–20 … 5–16 as cWW.
  - Seed partners in 7REX: 1–21 … 5–17, with **no** raw FR3D line for any of them.
  - STAR3D partners in 7REX: 1–22 … 5–18, all cWW, in chains A, B and C (U1–A22 in A and B only).
- **Shared-correspondence anchor fits.** Using 16 anchors (RMSD 2.64 Å), and 12 anchors when flanking residues are
  excluded (1.89 Å):
  - P1 3′ strand and L2 (6VUI 12–21; 3FU2 16–22): STAR3D partners 0.2–3.2 Å away, seed partners 4.1–8.6 Å.
  - L1 (6VUI G8, G11; 3FU2 G11): the **seed** partners are closer.
  - Only 6VUI A10 changes between anchor sets. The canonical-only anchor set was refused as degenerate.
- **Ligand context.** 7REX binds **two** preQ1 molecules, one of which contacts L1 (residues 7, 8, 12, 16). That
  plausibly changes L1's local geometry; the P1 helix register is not ligand-dependent.
- **Exploratory adjustment** (`results/EXPLORATORY_proposed_correspondences.tsv`,
  `review/candidate_RF00522/adjustment_validation.json`): shift 7REX A15–A22 one column left, putting A15 into insert
  column 24.
  - Nucleotides, order, row width and one-to-one mapping are all preserved.
  - SS_cons P1 pairs go from UC, GA, UC, GC, GC to UA, GC, UA, GC, GC.
  - On identical eligible sets, 6VUI→7REX: seed 19/46, **adjusted 31/46**, STAR3D 29/46 (canonical 0 / 5 / 5 of 7).
    3FU2→7REX: 20/44, **33/44**, 31/44.
  - No interaction preserved by the seed is lost.
  - L1 is deliberately left at the seed register.
- **Classification:** supported candidate Rfam correspondence issue for the P1 3′ strand; possible STAR3D issue for L1.
  This is one finding observed in two comparisons, not two discoveries.

  - the 3FU2 equivalents

## 6. Limits
- Canonical-pair evidence is not independent of STAR3D, because STAR3D's preprocessing uses MC-Annotate WWc pairs.
- Consensus secondary structure alone cannot establish an error.
- The fresh rerun covers the preQ1 pairs only.
- STAR3D ran under QEMU emulation.
- FR3D labels its annotations as not finalized.
- Several construct papers could not be accessed.
- The cohort has 3 primary families, with pairs that share RNAs.
- No rates may be extrapolated.
