# Standard Rfam seed vs original STAR3D: pilot report

*Pilot cohort v1, prompt Version 2.0, analysis dated 2026-10-09. Prepared by an AI research agent (Claude); no human review has been done yet.*

We compared nucleotide correspondences in the standard Rfam seed release **15.1** with those generated by original
STAR3D from experimental structures. Standard seeds may already incorporate structural information. The separately
requested ordinary-versus-structure-curated seed comparison is deferred under the current user-approved scope.

## 1. Question and design
For two naturally different RNAs from the same Rfam family, the question is where the published seed alignment and
original STAR3D v1.2 (default parameters) assign different corresponding nucleotides, and what the base interactions
show about those differences.

The setup was as follows:
- **Reference.** The reference was the pinned `Rfam.seed.gz` (sha256 41f014f4…), and only rows already in that file
  were used.
- **Structures.** Each RNA was the family interval of one chain from the RCSB mmCIF, model 1, with author numbering.
- **Annotations.** Base pairs and stacks came from FR3D-python.
- **Registration.** Both methods were mapped into the same row-index system through a residue crosswalk that is
  hard-checked against the aligner input files.

The cohort was frozen before any STAR3D run (`metadata/cohort_v1_freeze.json`).

There are 21 screen-passing families that were never reviewed, so their eligibility is **unknown, not excluded**. The
number of eligible families across the whole database is therefore not established. Details are in
`results/family_inventory.tsv` and `metadata/inventory_stage_counts.json`.

**Why candidates fell out.** Construct engineering was the dominant bottleneck:
- The other 26 *T. tengcongensis* SAM-I entries are modified forms of the 2GIS construct: k-turn swaps, point
  mutants, and one extended protein-complex construct (3V7E).
- In THF and guanidine-I, noncanonical pairs were deliberately converted to Watson–Crick or tandem G·U.
- The cobalamin construct 4GMA replaces its P1/P2 5′ segment and part of P6.
- The *A. thaliana* TPP construct redesigns P1/P2/P3.

A second issue was database metadata errors:
- 4GMA is labelled "marine metagenome", although the papers identify it as *T. tengcongensis*.
- 4GXY has no organism recorded; BLAST identifies *S. thermophilum*.
- The 4NYB/C/D/G seed `#=GR` features sit on a row whose sequence they do not match.

"Assessable" means both residues are observed and neither is a masked engineered position. Every run is in
`results/pair_summary.tsv`, and the residue-level rows are in `results/correspondence_comparison.tsv`.

**Determinism and direction.**
- Replicates were identical within each direction.
- For two pairs, forward and reverse runs differed by 2 pairs; for the other four with comparable runs they were identical.
- One run crashed under emulation (JVM SIGILL, recorded).

**Controls.** For SAM-I and cobalamin, STAR3D aligned only one helical module (25–26 nt). Positive controls show the
tool and inputs are sound: 2GIS vs 3IQR aligned 94/94 at RMSD 0.63 Å, and the self-alignments were complete. These
short alignments are therefore **STAR3D outcomes, not evidence about the seed.** In SAM-I, the module STAR3D chose
(P3) is exactly the engineered region of both constructs.

## 5. Inspected regions (all 19 candidates are listed in `results/region_review.tsv`)
1. **PreQ1-I, 7REX row, P1 3′ strand and L2 (supported candidate seed correspondence issue).** Four independent kinds
   of evidence converge:
   - **The seed's own consensus.** Read straight from the raw strings, `SS_cons` applied to the 7REX row implies
     U1·C21, G2·A20 and U3·C19, which are non-Watson–Crick (`review/RF00522_sscons_pairing_check.txt`).
   - **The 7REX structure.** FR3D shows U1–A22, G2–C21, U3–A20, G4–C19 and G5–C18, all cWW.
   - **Base-pair preservation.** The seed mapping preserves 0/5 of 6VUI's P1 Watson–Crick pairs in 7REX; STAR3D
     preserves 5/5, plus two A-minor cSW contacts.
   - **Method-neutral geometry.** After a fit on the 16 residues where both methods agree (RMSD 2.64 Å), STAR3D's
     partners lie 0.6–3.2 Å away and the seed's 6.1–8.6 Å.

   The 3FU2→7REX pair shows the same pattern. Both pairs implicate one row, so this is one finding observed twice.

   The construct is native (34/34 against the *C. antarcticum* genome), the link is verified, and nothing is masked.
   A one-position shift of 7REX residues 17–22 is proposed **only** in `results/EXPLORATORY_proposed_correspondences.tsv`;
   the seed file is never modified. Figures: `figures/fig_RF00522_6VUI_7REX_distances.png`,
   `figures/fig_RF00522_3FU2_7REX_distances.png`, `figures/fig_RF00522_6VUI_7REX_P1.png` (+ `.pse` and `.pml`).
2. **PreQ1-I L1, 6VUI 8–11 / 3FU2 9–12 (possible STAR3D correspondence issue).** The same neutral fit favours the
   seed partners (2.9–3.8 Å vs up to 10 Å).
3. **SAM-I whole pair (possible STAR3D issue; not interpretable for motifs).** STAR3D aligned only P3, the engineered
   region of both constructs.
4. **Agreement example: TPP 2GDI 70–75 ↔ 3D2G 67–72.** Both methods agree, at 0.5–2 Å after the neutral fit. The single
   2GDI A76 difference favours STAR3D geometrically (0.87 vs 7.35 Å) but lies beside a redesigned construct end, so it
   is not interpreted.

## 6. Limits
- This is a pilot: 3 primary families and 7 pairs, with pairs that share RNAs, so results are per family and no rates
  should be extrapolated.
- Constructs are pervasively engineered. Masking removes interpretation but not influence on STAR3D, which chose the
  engineered P3 in SAM-I.
- Several construct papers could not be accessed.
- STAR3D runs under QEMU emulation.
- FR3D-python labels its annotations as not finalized.
- STAR3D's preprocessing uses canonical pairs, so canonical preservation is not independent of STAR3D.
- A low RMSD or high preservation does not prove biological equivalence. The 7REX finding is a supported candidate,
  not a demonstrated error.
